Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a small private tool used by one creator to upload and schedule videos on their own YouTube channels:

  • Nuts, Bolts & Myths (youtube.com/@NutsBoltsMyths)
  • Who Else Is Awake? (youtube.com/@WhoElseIsAwake)
  • X-Ray Hangar (youtube.com/@X-RayHangar)

It runs on the creator's own computer. It reads the finished video file and its title, description and tags, uploads them with the YouTube Data API, sets the scheduled publish time and the thumbnail, and reads the video back to check that every field is correct.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access any other YouTube account.
